Research

Three connected strands, tied together by a focus on how teachers are formed and retained.

Theme

Teacher education dropout & career trajectories

Why pre-service and early-career teachers leave — and where they go instead — studied through longitudinal panel data.

Method

Longitudinal panel data analysis

Methodological work on tracking teacher education students and career changes over time, building on experience coordinating the Lehramtsstudierenden-Panel (LAP/NEPS) at LIfBi.

Featured

Digitalization in teacher education

VR simulations and AI in schools, including a habilitation project on self-regulated teaching in pre-service teachers using a VR classroom simulation.

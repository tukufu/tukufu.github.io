Teacher education research, written plainly.

Sebastian Franz is a postdoctoral researcher (Akademischer Rat a. Z.) at the Professur für Schulpädagogik, Katholische Universität Eichstätt-Ingolstadt. He holds a Dr. phil. in Empirical Educational Research from the Otto-Friedrich-Universität Bamberg, where he previously coordinated the Lehramtsstudierenden-Panel (LAP/NEPS) at LIfBi.

His research focuses on teacher education dropout and career trajectories, longitudinal panel data analysis, and the digitalization of teacher education — including VR simulations and AI in schools.
